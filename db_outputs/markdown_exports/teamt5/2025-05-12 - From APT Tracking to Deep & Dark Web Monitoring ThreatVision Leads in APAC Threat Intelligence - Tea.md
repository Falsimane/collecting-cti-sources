## Why APAC Intelligence Matters

The APAC region is home to a wide range of state-sponsored and cybercriminal actors that often deploy tactics and malware tailored specifically for local targets. Many of these threats are not captured by global feeds, leaving a critical blind spot for organizations in or interacting with the region. ThreatVision closes this gap by providing intelligence shaped by TeamT5’s 20+ years of deep focus on APAC cyber activities. Our research tracks APT groups, malware, and underground ecosystem trends—offering users timely, contextual insights that enable smarter, region-aware decision-making.

## Intelligence That Supports Every Layer and Role

ThreatVision delivers role-specific intelligence that supports security teams from strategy to incident response. Here’s how different users apply it:

**Decision-makers:**

  * Use APT Threat Landscape in Asia to stay informed on the overall threat landscape in APAC.

  * When reviewing current security measures or analyzing activities of adversaries, Campaign Tracking Report and Cyber Affairs Biweekly Report are key references.




**Risk Managers:**

  * Vulnerability Insights Reports (VIR) combines technical analysis with suggestions to help organizations focusing on exploited vulnerabilities and setting up effective remediations.

  * Patch Management Reports (PMR) help organizations to be notified timely of potentially exploitable vulnerabilities and prioritize patching to quickly mitigate emerging risks.




**Incident Responders:**

  * Apply Indicators of Compromise Feeds and the Malware Database to quickly identify related threats.

  * Use threat hunting tools and sample analysis features to facilitate threat response and triage time.

## Deep & Dark Web Monitoring: Reveal Threat Hidden in the Shadows

One of the most distinctive features of ThreatVision is its Deep & Dark Web (DDW) Monitoring service. Designed to uncover early warning signals and data leaks, which covers:

  * 1.5+ billion of leaked sensitive data

  * 30+ million compromised devices

  * 40+ deep and dark web forums and markets, including Chinese-speaking underground communities

  * 400+ monitored Telegram channels, where threat actors coordinate or distribute stolen data

  * Social media and forum activity relevant to cybercrime operations and breach chatter, especially in Chinese




What sets this apart is not just the scope of monitoring, but how it’s delivered—in one centralized, intuitive interface for easy browsing and searching. With weekly automated alerts and tailored analysis reports, organizations gain timely visibility into threats as they emerge, not after they escalate.
