# ThreatVision’s Threat Landscape: Grasp the Context Behind Critical Risks

2025.08.10TeamT5 Media Center

Share: 

In a rapidly changing cyber threat environment, it's often difficult for organizations to determine not whether an attack will occur, but rather **which attacks are actually relevant to them**. Most threat intelligence platforms only provide fragmented data, lacking context and relevance, making it difficult for security teams to determine which threats are the most pressing risks at the moment.

[ThreatVision](https://teamt5.org/en/products/threatvision/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=website)’s Threat Landscape was developed to solve this pain point. It provides a visual representation of correlated intelligence through an interactive hotspot map, integrating an overview of cyber attacks across different regions, countries, and industries. This helps organizations quickly grasp critical risks and clearly identify the threats they truly need to defend against.

![](https://uploads.teamt5.org/upload/original/EN_Overall_threatvision-s-threat-landscape-grasp-the-context-behind-critical-risks.png)

  


## Three Dimensions for Assessing Relevant Threats

ThreatVision’s Threat Landscape helps organizations fully understand global attack trends and strengthen their risk assessment and decision-making processes across three dimensions: **"Targeted Regions," "Targeted Countries," and "Targeted Industries."**

### 1\. Targeted Regions

The global map presents the overall attack landscape for each region and dynamically displays the relationship between attacking source countries and targeted regions. It also provides information on the countries covered, associated threat groups, and intelligence reports, allowing for in-depth tracking of attackers and threat activities related to that region.

![](https://uploads.teamt5.org/upload/original/EN_Targeted%20Regions_threatvision-s-threat-landscape-grasp-the-context-behind-critical-risks.gif)

### 2\. Targeted Countries

Click on any country on the map to dynamically display its affected status and primary attack sources. This also provides exclusive information on threat groups active in that country, the malicious tools they use, and the industries they target, allowing you to quickly grasp the overall threat landscape of that country.

![](https://uploads.teamt5.org/upload/original/EN_Targeted%20Countries_threatvision-s-threat-landscape-grasp-the-context-behind-critical-risks.png)

### 3\. Targeted Industries

For critical industries such as telecommunications, finance, and energy, it provides an overview of affected situations, associated groups, and malware-related information. Each industry page features a word cloud of keywords, making it easy to search for threat sources, tactics, and techniques, and quickly grasp the main risks for each industry.

![](https://uploads.teamt5.org/upload/original/EN_Targeted%20Industries_threatvision-s-threat-landscape-grasp-the-context-behind-critical-risks.png)

## From a Broad Overview to Practical Defense

ThreatVision’s Threat Landscape is not just a portal for overview information—it's the starting point for defense decisions. It provides actionable guidance tailored to different roles and task requirements:

  * **Information Security Team** : Quickly search for relevant intelligence reports based on your region and industry, and obtain related Indicators of Compromise (IoCs) and deployable detection tools.

  * **Incident Response Team** : Quickly understand information on attack groups and their Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures (TTPs) related to an incident, improving investigation and response efficiency.

  * **Security Decision-Makers** : Get a complete overview of both the overall and regional threat landscapes relevant to operations, allowing you to adjust defense priorities and resource allocation.




All information in the Threat Landscape is regularly updated and verified by the TeamT5 analyst team, ensuring the content is credible, timely, and can be practically applied to defense actions.
