# More Isn't Better—Organizations Need Relevant Local Threat Intelligence

2025.08.05Product Management

Share: 

Faced with an endless stream of cybersecurity threats, enterprises and organizations receive large volumes of threat intelligence from various sources every day—blacklisted IPs, malicious URLs, suspicious behaviors, attack techniques, and more. While this may seem comprehensive, it often turns out to be fragmented and difficult to manage.

The real question is: can this intelligence truly help enterprises and organizations prevent attacks that are specifically targeting them?

From our observations, what enterprises and organizations truly need is not more intelligence, but more accurate and relevant local threat insights.

  


## Overloaded Intelligence ≠ Effective Defense

Information security teams in enterprises and organizations often spend significant time screening intelligence sources, deduplicating, comparing, and reformatting data—only to discover that attacks specifically targeting their industry have already progressed to the lateral movement stage.

This highlights a key challenge in modern defense: while intelligence may be abundant, it often fails to accurately reflect real, relevant risks. The data is too fragmented and disorganized, making it difficult to translate into effective action.

  


## We Deliver a Different Kind of Intelligence – Local Threat Insights

Our team continuously tracks targeted advanced persistent threats (APTs) in the Asia-Pacific region and transforms these intelligence into:

  * Actionable detection rules tailored for specific industries such as manufacturing, finance, and technology

  * Attack technique analysis mapped to the MITRE ATT&CK framework

  * Actionable recommendations that include context, attacker behavior, and intent analysis

  * STIX and CSV format data that can be easily integrated into defense systems like EDR


  


This is more than just a warning sign—it’s threat intelligence that enterprises can truly apply.

  


## Make Faster Decisions with More Accurate Intelligence

Whether you're part of a CSIRT team, a MDR provider, or a security manager conducting threat risk assessments, our localized intelligence can help you:

  * **Reduce time** spent on threat identification

  * **Quickly** correlate attackers, their tactics, and related intelligence

  * **Focus** on the key risks most relevant to your organization
