## Why Choose Our APT Intelligence?

#### Focusing on Frontline Attack Intelligence in Asia Pacific

With years of deep engagement across the Asia Pacific region, we have tracked hundreds of APT groups—their infrastructure, behavioral patterns, and emerging tools.

#### Widely Applicable, Actionable Intelligence

Beyond static reports, we deliver indicators of compromise (IoCs) verified by our professional analyst team, along with specialized threat hunting tools designed for APT and vulnerability-based attacks. Our intelligence is high-quality, precise, and highly relevant—empowering your platform to help customers defend against and respond to threats more effectively.

#### Supports Standard Formats, Seamless API Integration

We support STIX and CSV formats, as well as API connectivity for easy integration with your threat intelligence platform, maximizing operational efficiency.

#### Not Just Reports—With Context for Smarter Decisions

Our intelligence is validated by experts and presented in a clear, structured, and contextualized format. This enables faster correlation of information, helping customers understand the background and rationale of each attack and make better-informed decisions.
