# Raw Intel’s Role in Modern Cyber Threat Intelligence

Staying ahead of emerging threats is paramount. At TeamT5, our dedicated analysts work tirelessly to research and translate findings into comprehensive threat intelligence reports. However, there are instances where traditional reports are not what customers are looking for. This is where Raw Intel comes in, offering invaluable real-time insights for cyber threat analysts.

Raw Intel serves as a crucial tool in our arsenal, providing users with detailed information gleaned from ongoing research efforts. When conducting searches on [ThreatVision](https://teamt5.org/en/products/threatvision/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=website), users may encounter Raw Intel objects associated with specific IP addresses, domains, or hashes. These objects represent a snapshot of our analysts' research within a certain timeframe, offering insights into targeted industries, employed malware, and adversary profiles.

Please note that Raw Intel is the preliminary results of TeamT5’s latest research. If you need further investigation and analysis to obtain the most accurate information, please [contact us](\(https://teamt5.org/en/contact-us/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=website).

Raw Intel empowers [ThreatVision](https://teamt5.org/en/products/threatvision/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=website) customers with three major advantages:

## Real-Time Response Capability

In the ever-evolving landscape of cyber threats, timing is critical. Threat intelligence reports, while valuable, may entail a delay due to human review processes. Raw Intel bridges this gap, empowering organizations to self-identify threats in near real-time. By leveraging Raw Intel, threat analysts can proactively respond to emerging threats, minimizing the risk of being caught off guard during attacks.

## Granular Threat Visibility

Raw Intel offers a granular view of potential threats, equipping security teams with detailed information about malicious IP addresses and samples. This level of insight enables precision in threat identification, facilitating the development of targeted defense strategies. With Raw Intel, organizations can mitigate the risk of false positives and enhance overall cybersecurity posture.

In conclusion, Raw Intel serves as a cornerstone in modern cyber threat analysis, offering real-time insights and granular visibility into emerging threats. By leveraging this invaluable resource, organizations can bolster their defenses and stay one step ahead in the ongoing battle against cyber adversaries.

## Access to Intel Regardless of Subscribed Items

Since ThreatVision’s Raw Intel is not dependent on the user being subscribed to any particular report, users are able to easily access snippets of intelligence that are important to them and their investigation even if they are not subscribed to the particular report that the intel is from.
