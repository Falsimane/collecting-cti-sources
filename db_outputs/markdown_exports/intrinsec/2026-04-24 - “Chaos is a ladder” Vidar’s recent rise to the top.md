# “Chaos is a ladder”: Vidar’s recent rise to the top

- **Source :** intrinsec
- **URL :** https://www.intrinsec.com/en/chaos-is-a-ladder-vidar-rise-to-the-top/
- **Date :** 2026-04-24

---

The year 2025 saw many shifts in the stealer ecosystem, mainly due to international police operations leading to the **takedowns of Lumma and Rhadamanthys infrastructure**. However, the chaos resulting from their disruption created more opportunities for competitors, including **Vidar**.

#### Key findings

  * The state of Vidar in the beginning of 2026. Following **major takedowns affecting Lumma and Rhadamanthys** , Vidar took advantage of the generated chaos to rise to the top of the stealer ecosystem. We assess that this rise was made available due to the **release of version 2.0 of the malware, and to the collaboration with “Cloud” Telegram channels**.
  * Details on a kill-chain that infected corporate employees. We were able to analyze the whole kill-chain starting from the **download of a fake software advertised on YouTube, unpacking of the Vidar stealer and selling of credentials on Russian Market**.
  * Analysis of the Vidar sample. While the new version of the malware was already recently analyzed by other editors, we gave details on the C2 recovery mechanism using **dead drop resolvers, control flow flattening to slow down analysis**. The discovery of an **unpacked sample potentially uploaded by a Russian threat actor** on VirusTotal reveals the classic stealing capabilities.
  * Details on the infrastructure used by Vidar. This infrastructure was previously identified in our Acreed analysis; however, we were able to determine the **use of some of the domains for the generation of built payloads** by clients of Vidar.
