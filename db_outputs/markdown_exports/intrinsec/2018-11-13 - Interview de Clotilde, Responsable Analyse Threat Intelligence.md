**Hello Clotilde, can you tell us about the Cyber Threat Intelligence solution?**

**Clotilde:** In a few words, the **Cyber Threat Intelligence** is a service based on an approach to **cyber intelligence** , which allows us to anticipate and detect threats across all layers of the Internet (Surface, Deep and Dark).

**The Threat Intelligence cell** operates at both strategic and technical levels. On the one hand, it allows for **provide information on the attacker/attacked contexts** And **sector-specific monitoring reports** , by understanding the specific context of the company.

On the other hand,**It addresses fundamental issues for businesses** , By identifying threats with rapid operational leverage: combating data leaks and fraud, protecting information systems, personal data, and ultimately, brand image. This offering goes hand in hand with the SOC, as it allows for the observation and analysis of what is happening outside the information system, with the aim of detecting incidents and helping companies respond.

Beyond the**continuous dimension of the service,** This approach can be used on an ad hoc basis to take stock of the situation or to support other initiatives (risk assessment in the context of an acquisition, request for information to support an internal investigation, etc.).
