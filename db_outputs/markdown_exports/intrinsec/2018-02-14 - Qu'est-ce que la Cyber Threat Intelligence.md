## **What is Cyber Threat Intelligence?**

![Cyber Threat Intelligenc](https://www.intrinsec.com/wp-content/uploads/alessio-lin-208182-unsplash-1.jpg)

In a few words, the **Cyber Threat Intelligence** is a service based on a cyber intelligence approach, which allows’**to anticipate and detect threats across all layers of the Internet** (Surface, Deep and Dark). The Threat Intelligence unit operates at both strategic and technical levels.

On the one hand, it allows us to provide information on the **attacker/attacked contexts** and sector-specific monitoring reports, by identifying the company's specific context. On the other hand, it addresses fundamental challenges for businesses by identifying threats with rapid operational leverage: combating **data leaks** and the **fraud** , protection of the information system, personal data and finally of the’**brand image**. This offering goes hand in hand with the SOC, as it allows for the observation and analysis of what is happening outside the information system, with the aim of detecting incidents and...’**help businesses respond**.

Beyond the ongoing nature of the service, this approach can be used on an ad hoc basis to carry out a **state****places** or to support other initiatives (risk assessment in the context of an acquisition, request for information to support an internal investigation, etc.)

## **Who is Cyber Threat Intelligence aimed at?**

Cyber Threat Intelligence is a powerful solution, primarily aimed at organizations with **a major exhibition****** : a large technological footprint, information assets to protect, a large number of employees, or a substantial supply chain.
