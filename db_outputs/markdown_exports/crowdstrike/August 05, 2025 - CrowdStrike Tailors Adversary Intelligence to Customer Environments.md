# CrowdStrike Tailors Adversary Intelligence to Customer Environments

## Falcon Adversary Intelligence embeds real-time, personalized threat insights directly into analyst workflows, transforming how organizations access, prioritize, and act on intelligence.

August 05, 2025

• [ Thuy Nguyen](/en-us/blog/author.thuy-nguyen/ "Posts by Thuy Nguyen") • [Threat Hunting & Intel](/en-us/blog/category.counter-adversary-operations/ "Threat Hunting & Intel")


A new release of CrowdStrike Falcon® Adversary Intelligence delivers automatically prioritized threat intelligence tailored to each unique customer environment. By surfacing the right intelligence at the right time, this update enhances SOC workflows by enabling faster triage, deeper investigations, and more confident response.

Today’s organizations understand threat intelligence is critical to stay ahead of adversaries, but many struggle to determine which threats matter most and how to act on them. SOC teams are overwhelmed by noisy data and constrained by fragmented tools that force analysts to manually stitch together investigations, wasting time and increasing the risk of missed threats. 

This release of Falcon Adversary Intelligence addresses this problem by turning static data into real-time, operational advantage.

## Introducing Personalized Threat Intelligence

CrowdStrike now delivers prioritized and customized intelligence based on an organization’s industry, infrastructure, and exposure. We understand every customer’s unique environment and use this knowledge to tailor intelligence to each one. 

Falcon Adversary Intelligence flags threats observed in each customer’s environment so analysts understand which are most urgent to address. It reports on relevant threats and trends, tracks activity on the dark web, and surfaces the most relevant information based on an organization’s industry, tech stack, and detections. Intelligence is escalated when tied to customer-specific risk factors, such as an adversary that has been targeting their industry or has been seen in their specific environment. This tailored information improves triage decisions and accelerates response to the most relevant threats.

## Intelligence Explorer and Indicator App

Two new capabilities are built to accelerate investigations: Intelligence Explorer and the Indicator App.

Intelligence Explorer unifies CrowdStrike’s comprehensive intelligence into a centralized workspace where analysts can discover and investigate threats across different indicators, malware, adversaries, and vulnerabilities. The tool surfaces relationships between threats so analysts can view connections between threat actors, their techniques, and affected assets. 

Armed with this information, analysts can shift from triage to deep investigation. Intelligence Explorer empowers teams with the context they need to accelerate threat research and take action — all in a single intuitive interface.

The Indicator App is another powerful investigative tool. Analysts can directly pivot from urgent indicators of compromise (IOCs) into the Indicator App, which delivers contextual detail around every IOC. The app includes links to related adversaries, malware, vulnerabilities, and threat reports, as well as timeline views of when and where indicators were observed, integrated kill chain overlays, and entity relationships. 

This enhanced workflow turns every indicator into actionable intelligence so teams can conduct smarter, faster investigations.

## Powered by Industry-Leading Intelligence 

CrowdStrike is redefining how threat intelligence is used. This release delivers customized insights based on our industry-leading adversary intelligence and each organization’s environment, industry, and risk. By surfacing the most relevant and actionable threats, Falcon Adversary Intelligence empowers analysts to stop them with confidence.

CrowdStrike has long set the standard for world-class threat intelligence, and we continue to innovate. The latest release of Falcon Adversary Intelligence uses the industry’s deepest understanding of adversary tradecraft to change how organizations receive and use threat intelligence. It delivers threat intelligence relevant to each customer’s environment, risk profile, and priorities, helping ensure the right information reaches the right teams at the right time. 

All existing Falcon Adversary Intelligence customers will automatically receive these updates as part of their current subscription.

Whether used as a standalone solution or integrated across the CrowdStrike Falcon® platform, Falcon Adversary Intelligence modules give organizations the ability to reduce noise, improve analyst efficiency, and proactively respond, turning threat intelligence into meaningful outcomes.
