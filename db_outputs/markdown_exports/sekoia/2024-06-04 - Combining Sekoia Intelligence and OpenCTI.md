# Combining Sekoia Intelligence and OpenCTI

- **Source :** sekoia
- **URL :** https://www.sekoia.com/blog/combining-sekoia-intelligence-and-opencti
- **Date :** 2024-06-04

---

The [**Filigran**](https://filigran.io/)**x Sekoia** partnership announcement is an opportunity to put the spotlight back on the benefits of the integration between OpenCTI and Sekoia Threat Intelligence.

The existing Sekoia connector for OpenCTI has been released in 2021 and is freely available on the OpenCTI Github repository. This connector is regularly updated and maintained by Filigran.

The Integration between OpenCTI and Sekoia.io is a data import connector. This connector leverages the ingestion of **new intelligence from Sekoia.io into an OpenCTI instance with a 100% preservation of source data** based on a native STIX 2.1 support on both sides.

Want to know more on how the integration works? Read more on [Filigran blog](https://blog.filigran.io/sekoia-io-threat-intelligence-in-opencti-2fa8a32c5247).
