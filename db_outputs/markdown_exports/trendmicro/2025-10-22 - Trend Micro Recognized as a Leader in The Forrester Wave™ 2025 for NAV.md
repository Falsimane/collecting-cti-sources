**Our Take on Why Trend Micro is a Leader in NAV  
Unified security. Industry-recognized performance.**

Excellent news: we’re proud to share that Trend has been named **a Leader** in **The Forrester Wave™: Network Analysis and Visibility Solutions, Q4 2025**. To us, this recognition is more than a milestone; and reflects years of focused investment in helping organizations simplify complexity, accelerate investigations, and make decisions that reduce real-world risk. 

![forrester wave](https://www.trendmicro.com/content/dam/trendmicro/global/en/research/25/j/forrester-wave-nav-2025/forrester-wave.png)

Our vision for network analysis and visibility (NAV) has always been clear

Security and network teams don’t just need alerts; they need actionable intelligence delivered in real time. That’s where Trend Vision One™ comes in. Our unified AI-powered enterprise cybersecurity platform enables you to combine advanced network telemetry ingestion, extended detection and response (XDR), and cyber risk exposure management, delivering end-to-end visibility and control.

Trend Vision One™ Cyber Risk Exposure Management (CREM) empowers your organization to proactively uncover, assess, and mitigate threats, so you can reduce exposure and build cyber resilience. At the same time, network analytics and visibility (NAV) give you the foundational data that both security operations center (SOC) and network operations center (NOC) teams rely on to monitor, investigate, and respond to threats and operational issues.

Together, these capabilities create a seamless SOC and NOC experience, enabling your teams to anticipate threats, gain visibility across every environment, and act with speed and precision.

Recent investments in agentic SIEM have further strengthened this foundation for NAV. By seamlessly ingesting third-party network telemetry and integrating with XDR, you can expand the reach of our NAV capabilities without forcing your organization to rip and replace existing systems. The focus is on a unified SOC and NOC platform that scales visibility, correlates signals, and streamlines workflows for analysts at every level.

With proper visibility, you can drive proactive decisions

In today’s complex threat landscape, visibility is power. Your security and network teams must balance proactive strategies that reduce risk with reactive capabilities that accelerate response. CREM enables both.

  * Proactive: Eliminate blind spots and prioritize risk
    *     * Uncover unmanaged assets and blind spots across East-West and North-South traffic
    * Gain comprehensive network visibility and asset-level intelligence to enable risk-based prioritization and measurable reduction in exposure
  * Reactive: Accelerate detection and response
    *     * When incidents occur, CREM provides the telemetry and context needed for you to detect, investigate, and respond quickly
    * Leverage incident response across all network assets by correlating detections and surfacing high-fidelity alerts
